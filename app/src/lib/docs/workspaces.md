# Workspaces, groups, members and backups

A workspace holds diagrams and the people who can see them. Everyone has a **Personal** workspace, only ever theirs.

![The home screen: workspaces on the left, the diagrams in one as cards](/app/docs-images/home.png)


- **+ New workspace** on the home screen makes one; you're its owner.
- **Manage** by its name: rename it, add people by email (they can be added before they first sign in), remove them, export a backup, add [types](/app/docs/types-and-icons), or delete it with all its diagrams.
- Members see every diagram in it and can copy them. A diagram is changed only by its owner, the person who made it.
- Only a workspace's owner sees everyone in it; a member sees the owner and themselves. People are added by email, without being asked first, so add only people who expect it.
- A member can **leave** a workspace; what they made there passes to its owner.
- A diagram's owner can **move** it to another workspace of theirs from its sheet, with its history.
- Limits, far above everyday use: 100 people in a workspace, 50 workspaces and 1000 diagrams of your own.

## Groups

A workspace's diagrams can be filed under **groups**: a C4 set, a service, a team, whatever keeps them findable.

- The **folder button** on a card of yours (shown on hover; always on a touch screen), or **Group** in a diagram's sheet, files it: pick a group in use, type a new one, or choose **No group**. A group exists while a diagram is in it.
- With groups in a workspace, its page shows the cards under a heading per group, the ones in no group last. The row of groups under the search keeps the list to one group, or to **No group**; on a phone it scrolls sideways.
- A search, or one group, lists the cards flat, each naming its group.
- **+ New diagram** can file it straight away, in the group on show to start with. A new C4 level made from **Zoom in** or **Zoom out** joins the group of the diagram it came from, and so does a duplicate in the same workspace.
- Groups are flat and belong to the workspace, not to the diagram: they aren't in its history or its config, and a diagram moved to another workspace is in no group there. Only a diagram's owner files it.

## Backups

**Export a backup** (in Manage) downloads the workspace as one file: its diagrams with their groups, their snapshots, which follows which, and its types (not its members). **Import a backup…** on the home screen turns each workspace in a file into a new one of yours. A workspace over 64 MB with its history is too big for one file: move some diagrams to another workspace and export each.
