# Getting started

Skope draws how your systems fit together: the services, databases, queues and people, the connections between them, and the boxes they live in. A diagram is a document you can edit by hand on the canvas, as a YAML or JSON config, or through an AI connected to Skope.

## Your first diagram

1. On the home screen, pick a workspace on the left (everyone starts with **Personal**) and press **+ New diagram**.
2. Give it a name. Start from a **blank canvas** or a template (C4 Container, C4 Context, Deployment topology or Freeform), with or without its example nodes.
3. On the canvas, press **+ Add** in the toolbar and pick a type, say **Service**. It lands in free space and opens its details on the right.
4. Type a name. Add a second node, then drag the **+** port on the first one onto the second to connect them.
5. That's it: every change saves as you go. ⌘Z undoes and ⇧⌘Z redoes.

## Where things are


![The editor: views and tags on the left, the canvas with its toolbar in the middle, History on the right](/app/docs-images/editor.png)


- **Home** lists your workspaces (yours, then those shared with you) and the diagrams in one. Search finds diagrams by their name or the names of their nodes.
- **The canvas** is the diagram. Drag to pan, pinch or ctrl+scroll to zoom. The toolbar over it adds and connects things.
- **The left panel** holds views and tags: what you see. **The right panel** holds the details of what's selected, and the History.
- **The menu under your name** has your Profile, Appearance, and these docs.

On a phone the canvas takes the whole screen and the panels open as sheets from the bottom bar. See [Keyboard and touch](/app/docs/shortcuts).

![The editor on a phone: the canvas full screen, with Add, undo and redo at the top](/app/docs-images/phone-editor.png)
