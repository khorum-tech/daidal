# Connecting AI

Skope is getting an MCP server, so an AI on your computer (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, claude.ai) can list your diagrams, read them and make changes as you. This page will say how to connect one once it's ready.

Until then, the [config format](/app/docs/config) is the easiest way to have an AI draft a diagram: ask it for a Skope config, then paste it into **Config** and apply it.
