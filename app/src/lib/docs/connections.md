# Connections

A connection joins two nodes, or a node and a container.

## Making one

- Drag the **+** port on a selected node onto another node, or onto empty canvas to make a new node there.
- Or click the port, then the other end.
- Or pick **Connect** in the toolbar, then click one node and the other.
- On a phone: select the node, **Connect** in its menu, then tap the other end.

## Changing one

Click (or tap) a connection to open its details:


![A connection's details: label, arrows, weight, line, route and ends](/app/docs-images/connection-details.png)


- **Label**, and whether it shows on the line.
- **Arrows**: forward, back, both or none.
- **Weight** and **line**: solid, dashed or dotted.
- **Route**: curved, straight or angled.
- **Ends**: which side of each box the line leaves from. With a mouse, drag the handle on either end along the box to pin it (it snaps to the quarters); double-click it to put it back on auto.
- **Reverse** swaps its ends; **Delete** removes it.

Pick several connections (shift-click) to change their arrows, weight, line and labels together.
