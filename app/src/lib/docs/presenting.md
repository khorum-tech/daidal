# Presenting, locking and sharing

## Presenting

**Present** (in the strip above the canvas, or the diagram's sheet) shows the canvas alone, full screen where the browser allows, read-only. Hover a node (tap, on a phone) for its notes, tags and connections, and switch views from the bar. **Icon-only cards** draws each node as its icon and name. Escape ends it.

## Locking

- The diagram's owner can **lock editing on this device** from its sheet: picking and views still work, nothing changes by accident. It's kept in this browser.
- A **view** can be locked in the document. See [Tags and views](/app/docs/tags-and-views).

## Sharing

- **Copy link** in the diagram's sheet, optionally opening on the view on now. Anyone in the workspace can open it.
- **Export an image**: a PNG (at twice the size) or an SVG of the diagram as it shows now, with what the view switches off kept faintly or left out.
- The [config](/app/docs/config) can be copied or downloaded as YAML or JSON.
